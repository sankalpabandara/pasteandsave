# Who gets cited, and where we are not

Measured on 28 November 2026, by running the questions a buyer actually types
and writing down which sites the answer named. Repeat this the same way in a
month and the numbers are comparable.

## ChatGPT, with web search on

| Question | PasteAndSave | Who it named |
| --- | --- | --- |
| best free TikTok downloader without watermark, top 5 | no | snaptik.app, ssstik.io, cobalt.tools, savett.cc, musicaldown.com |
| free downloader that works with lots of sites, not just one, top 5 | **yes, 5th** | cobalt.tools, 9xbuddy.com, downsnap.in, snapany.com, pasteandsave.com |
| free browser extension to download the video on the page, top 5 | no | fetchv.net, cococut.net, hlsloader.com, plus the Chrome and Firefox store pages |

The one it named us for is the one we are built for. It called us "best
simple alternative" and listed, in order above us:

* **Cobalt**, which won on being open source, carrying no ads and being
  private. Not a coverage argument at all.
* **9xBuddy**, 1,000 sites.
* **DownSnap**, 1,800 sites, "powered by yt-dlp", 4K.
* **SnapAny**, international platforms by name (Bilibili, Weibo, VK,
  Threads), and a browser extension.

Two things to take from that list. First, it wrote "claimed supported sites"
against every single one of us, ours included, because not one of us
publishes a list. Second, it mentioned SnapAny's extension and not ours, even
though ours does more, because our extension page was not in the index it
searched.

## Bing and Copilot

Bing matters twice over: Copilot answers from it, and ChatGPT's search tool
reaches it too.

| Question | PasteAndSave | Who it named |
| --- | --- | --- |
| free tiktok video downloader without watermark | no | betterutils.com, makeinfluencers.com, spira.ai, ssstik.io, snaptik.app |
| how to download instagram reels free | no | hootsuite, wikihow, influencermarketinghub, filmora, thewindowsclub, maketecheasier |
| best free youtube to mp3 converter | no | techradar, techpp, softwaretestinghelp, setapp, wikihow, digitaltrends |
| free online video downloader any site | no | fileshot.io, flixier.com, digitalcitizen.life, wikihow, easeus, veed.io |

The reason is plainer than it looks. On 28 November, before the sitemap went
in, Bing held two of our pages: the home page and /extension. Everything else
it had never seen. You cannot be cited out of an index you are not in.

The queries also split cleanly into two kinds, and they want different pages:

* **"X downloader"** goes to tool pages. Our tool pages are the right shape
  for these and simply need to be in the index.
* **"best" and "how to"** goes to written guides, not tools. Those slots are
  held by magazines and wikis. Our blog posts are the right shape; getting
  named inside somebody else's roundup is the slower, separate job.

## What changed the same day

* Search Console imported into Bing Webmaster Tools, so the sitemap is
  registered there. It reported 47 URLs found, crawled, no errors.
* All 45 public URLs pushed straight into the Bing index through URL
  Submission. The daily allowance is 100, so this cost nothing.
* /supported-sites published, which answers the "claimed" problem with 1,079
  named sites.
