## Diagnosis

Triage row: **"Key pages not indexed, or near zero impressions"** (discovery and indexing). Nothing else matters until this moves.

Likely causes, in order:
1. **A new, unlinked domain in a crowded niche** [P]. DR 0 against DR 57 to 80 competitors. Google crawls the tool pages rarely and has not indexed any blog post. Blog posts that are crawled and not indexed after a month on a low authority site is the usual pattern.
2. **No feedback loop** [E, own data]. GSC is not connected, Bing Webmaster Tools unknown, IndexNow broken, and the in-house crawler skips the blog. Nobody has been told the blog is not indexed.
3. **Head terms are out of reach for now** [P]. "tiktok video downloader" and similar are held by DR 70+ sites. Long tail and AI answers are where a new site can win first, and the ChatGPT referrals already show that.

## Overlooked opportunities

1. **ChatGPT is already the best search channel** [E, own data]. 24 tool page visits from chatgpt.com vs about 1 from Google in 14 days, and OAI-SearchBot crawls more than Googlebot. Run the cross engine check monthly: ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google "best free reddit video downloader with sound", "download X photos without login", "video to mp3 converter no install" and note which sites and which sentences get cited.
2. **First hand data nobody else publishes** [E source, P effect]. `events.jsonl` knows which platforms work right now. A monthly "what works today" table (success rate per platform, known blockers such as Vimeo embed only, Facebook share links, region age checks) is unique, linkable and refreshes itself. Use real counts only.
3. **Fixes content** [P]. Your failing lookup codes (LOGIN_ONLY, DRM_PROTECTED, EMBED_ONLY, AGE_CHECK_REGION) are exactly the questions users search. Short answer first sections under each tool page beat another generic guide.
4. **Overlooked languages** [P]. Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian and Hindi demand for downloaders is large and competitors translate badly. Already half built.
5. **Long tail over head terms** [P]. "reddit video downloader with sound", "x photo downloader", "linkedin video downloader", "threads video downloader" have weaker SERPs than TikTok or YouTube.
